About FreshCheck

FreshCheck is a specialized apple freshness classification interface designed to minimize food waste and support healthier eating habits. Using real-time vision algorithms, FreshCheck inspects skin texture, firmness, browning, and decay patterns.

All processing runs purely client-side within your browser, ensuring zero camera data is ever sent to external cloud servers.

🍎 Preservation Tip:

Store apples in the crisper drawer of your refrigerator away from vegetables to keep them crisp for up to 6–8 weeks.
